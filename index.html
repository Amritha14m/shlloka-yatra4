The pilgrimage

Walk towards Kailash, prepared and well supported.

The Kailash Mansarovar Parikrama is a sacred journey from Kathmandu to Lake Manasarovar and around Mount Kailash. We plan it end to end, so you can focus on what matters: the spiritual experience.

Sacred destinationsPashupatinath, Lake Manasarovar and the three-day Kailash Parikrama.
Experienced support teamYatra leader, Nepali guides and a dedicated support crew.
Safe and well-supportedHealth checks, oxygen, medical support and safety backup.
Meaningful experienceA spiritually focused itinerary that respects local culture and traditions.

Important information

Inclusions, exclusions, cancellations and participation notes.

What the yatra cost includes
  • Tibet Travel Permit and Tibet / China visa fee
  • Kathmandu sightseeing, havan and puja
  • Accommodation in Kathmandu and route hotels or guesthouses
  • Dormitory-style guesthouse accommodation at Diraphuk and Zutulphuk during the Parikrama
  • Pure vegetarian meals during the yatra
  • All ground transportation as per itinerary
  • Professional yatra leader and experienced support crew
  • Medical support service during the yatra
  • Duffel bag, water bottle holder, hanger, rain poncho, fleece hat and sun cap
  • Oxygen support during the yatra
  • Drinking water or mineral water during the yatra
  • For Options 2 and 3, the applicable Kathmandu–Lhasa and Lhasa–Ali/Pulan flight sectors
  • Nandi and Ashtapad darshan are subject to government regulations and only from the government-specified viewing points
What the yatra cost does not include
  • International and domestic flights other than the flight sectors in the selected option
  • Nepal visa fee, if applicable
  • Medical and travel insurance, which must cover emergency air and land evacuation
  • Medical treatment expenses
  • Horse and porter charges during the Kailash Parikrama
  • Extra hotel nights in Kathmandu due to early arrival, late departure or cancellation caused by unusual circumstances
  • Hotel accommodation at Darchen if a yatri does not continue for Parikrama and returns from Diraphuk
  • Vehicle cost if taken from any point during the Parikrama to exit the Parikrama before completion
  • Personal expenses of any kind
  • Helicopter costs if separately required
  • Additional expenses caused by natural calamities, political disturbances, vehicle malfunction, flight cancellation or other unusual circumstances
Cancellation policy
  • 25% refund before 75 days from the start of the program
  • 50% refund before 60 days from the start of the program
  • No refund within 30 days of the start of the program
  • If you are unable to attend the program, there will be no refunds and no re-registration for this
  • If you leave the program for any reason, there will be no refund
  • Online payments are refunded to the same mode of payment, via Google Pay (QR code), NEFT, IMPS or bank account. The money is credited to the respective bank account. Please allow up to 4 weeks to process the refund
  • There will be no refund for a no-show
Important participation notes
  • Travel to remote religious sites requires flexibility. Weather, crowd control, local authority directions, permit issues or sudden closures may require immediate changes to the itinerary, route, timing or accommodation.
  • This journey involves inherent risks including high altitude, strenuous activity, difficult terrain, unpredictable weather, limited medical access and possible evacuation delays.
  • Participants should consult a qualified medical professional and ensure they are physically fit for the yatra.
  • Services and schedules are subject to conditions beyond the direct control of Shlloka Yatra Private Limited.
  • Participants should read inclusions and exclusions carefully and disclose any special requirements in advance.
