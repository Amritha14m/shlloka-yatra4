Important information

What is included, and what to know before you book.

What the yatra cost includes
  • Tibet Travel Permit and Tibet / China visa fee
  • Kathmandu sightseeing, havan and puja
  • Accommodation in Kathmandu and route hotels or guesthouses
  • Dormitory-style guesthouse at Diraphuk and Zutulphuk during the Parikrama
  • Pure vegetarian meals during the yatra
  • All ground transportation as per itinerary
  • Professional yatra leader and experienced support crew
  • Medical support, oxygen support, duffel bag, rain poncho and fleece hat
  • For Options 2 and 3, the Kathmandu–Lhasa and Lhasa–Ali/Pulan flight sectors
What it does not include
  • International and domestic flights other than those in your package
  • Nepal visa fee, if applicable
  • Medical and travel insurance, which must cover emergency air and land evacuation
  • Horse and porter charges during the Parikrama
  • Extra hotel nights in Kathmandu for early arrival, late departure or cancellation caused by unusual circumstances
  • Personal expenses and helicopter costs if required
  • Costs caused by natural calamities, political disturbances, vehicle malfunction, flight cancellation or other unusual circumstances
Booking and payment terms
  • Register with a booking amount of INR 25,000 or US$ 250 per person. This is non-refundable.
  • 50% payment to confirm the yatra.
  • The balance 50% is payable 30 days after the first payment.
  • The deposit forms part of the initial booking payment.
Cancellation policy
  • 25% refund before 75 days from the start of the program
  • 50% refund before 60 days from the start of the program
  • No refund within 30 days of the start
  • No refund if you leave the program for any reason, or for a no-show
  • Refunds go to the original payment source and take up to 4 weeks to process
